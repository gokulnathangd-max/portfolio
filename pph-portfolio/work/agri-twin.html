In Progress Project

Agri-Twin

A predictive 3D digital twin platform engineered to prevent post-harvest loss through spatial facility visualization and telemetry monitoring.

Problem

Post-harvest agricultural storage facilities experience significant product spoilage and crop loss caused by micro-climate variations—such as localized moisture pockets, heat buildup, and stagnant airflow.

Standard numerical logs and 2D charts fail to communicate the three-dimensional reality of storage silos and warehouses. Facility managers cannot easily pinpoint which spatial quadrant is deteriorating before physical damage occurs.

What I Built

Agri-Twin (currently in progress) is an interactive 3D digital twin that models storage facilities directly in the web browser. The system processes sensor telemetry and maps environmental conditions onto an accurate 3D spatial representation.

Core functional components include:

  • Interactive Three.js 3D viewport with OrbitControls for rotating and slicing storage models
  • High-throughput backend service built on Fastify and TypeScript for fast telemetry processing
  • Visual heatmap indicators overlaid onto 3D storage meshes to flag risk thresholds
  • Modular React frontend architecture allowing operators to inspect individual storage sections

Stack

Fastify TypeScript React Three.js WebGL

Fastify was selected for its minimal overhead and high throughput when routing telemetry updates. Three.js delivers real-time hardware-accelerated 3D graphics in standard web browsers without browser extensions.

My Role

Designing and implementing the backend service in Fastify/TypeScript, writing the WebGL rendering logic in Three.js, and integrating the 3D scene controls into the React client application.

Additional Details:

Outcome

Currently in active development. The platform demonstrates the viability of browser-based 3D digital twins for industrial agriculture and storage management, providing actionable spatial insight to prevent preventable crop loss.
